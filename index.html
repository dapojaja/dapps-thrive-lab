About Me

My background sits at the intersection of business strategy and technical execution. I transitioned into AI-powered problem solving because I realized that the combination of large language models and modern automation frameworks could collapse traditional operational loops from weeks to minutes.

Through the **Attention Factory AI Fellowship**, I have developed a rigorous, hands-on understanding of how to configure LLMs, design scalable workflows in n8n, deploy private offline models, and build fully responsive applications.
